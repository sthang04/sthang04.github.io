Bae Irene (Red Velvet)

Leader of Red Velvet · Singer · Actress

About Irene

Bae Joo-hyun, professionally known as Irene, is a South Korean singer, rapper, and actress born on March 29, 1991. She debuted in August 2014 as the leader, visual, and main rapper of the popular K-pop girl group Red Velvet under SM Entertainment. In addition to group activities, she is part of the sub-unit Red Velvet - IRENE & SEULGI and made her film acting debut in the 2021 movie Double Patty.

Interesting Facts

  • Representative Color: Her designated color in Red Velvet is Pink.
  • Hometown: She was born in Daegu, South Korea.
  • Stage Name: Her stage name "Irene" is inspired by the Greek goddess of peace.
  • Acting Debut: She played the lead role of Lee Hyun-ji in the film Double Patty (2021).
  • Sub-Unit: She and Seulgi formed Red Velvet's first sub-unit in 2020 with the hit song "Monster".
